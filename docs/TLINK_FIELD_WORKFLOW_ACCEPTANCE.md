# TLink field workflow acceptance brief

Build a phone app that an assigned trade worker can use to complete the day's work without returning to the desktop portal. Keep one parent job for the customer and property, with an independent record for each selected activity. Creditex receives the signed field records and handles certificate creation with the program administrator.

## Required behaviour

- Create jobs in three short stages: customer/property, work selection, then worker/appointment. Always require an explicit worker choice, including a one-person business. Allow rental, STC and VEU work on the same job.
- Use one question at a time with Previous, Next, Sections and Job navigation. Show the camera on each photo question immediately. Retain answers and pending photos on the phone when a request fails.
- Default rental assessments to the six areas in Victoria's 2027 energy-efficiency changes. Keep current general minimum standards and specialist inspections separately selectable. Prefill the assigned worker's Team details. Ask for final declarations after the observations and evidence are complete.
- Make every current catalogue activity available as a field form. Keep closed activities out of new work. Capture relevant before-work information and declarations before installation details and final signatures.
- Use Creditex provider identity. Give customers access to applicable consumer documents and record actual provision. Never infer consent, delivery, a signature, GPS coordinates or an approved certificate quantity.
- Store activity answers, original uploads, capture metadata and signatures with the activity record. Produce an immutable completed PDF and an expiring, revocable report link. Preserve existing signed records when a master changes.
- Permit authorised AEA administrators and Creditex users to edit master forms. Saving immediately publishes the next default version. Team permissions control field evidence and commercial actions.
- Finish the trade activity when its required field record is submitted to Creditex. Certificate creation and administrator acceptance happen later in Creditex's workflow.

## Field-test sequence

1. Update and restart the preview app. Create a new rental job and explicitly choose a worker.
2. Complete the 2027 assessment: navigate all six categories, capture photos before answering, return to the job and reopen a saved draft. Check the final declaration appears after the inspection.
3. Create a mixed rental and certificate-activity job. Check each form has its own progress and evidence. Provide the customer handouts, enter test facts and use clearly identified test signatories.
4. Briefly disconnect during draft work, reconnect, and check retained photos upload once. Complete the field record and confirm it appears in the portal and Creditex review queue.
5. Open the completed PDF and report link, check photos and metadata, then revoke the test link. Confirm the overall job can finish once every required sub-record is complete.

Automated tests and native exports do not replace this physical-device camera, GPS and signature test. A field record is not proof that a certificate has been created or accepted by a program administrator.
