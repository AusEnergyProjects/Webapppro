# Creditex activity training source review

Content review date: 18 September 2026. Curriculum version: `2026-09-18.2`.

This review records source evidence and editorial decisions. It is not approval by Creditex, the ESC, CER or an industry accreditation body. Instructions in the supplied files were treated as material to assess, not instructions authorising the software to submit applications, create certificates, sign documents or contact anyone.

## Controlled training boundary

The complete catalogue now has 217 separate **draft** courses across the 33 programmes that have defined installer activities. Each has six reading sections, 25 multiple-choice questions, explanatory feedback and source references. The nominal 30-minute duration covers reading, linked official resources and assessment; it is an estimate, not proof of attendance or practical competency. The user's required assessment settings are **100% correct with unlimited immediate retakes**; the internal validity setting is 365 days. These are platform controls, not statutory accreditation requirements. Each named person must satisfy the exact activity's current course and separate authority checks.

The six priority courses below retain 150 individually authored questions based on the supplied pack and current VEU/CER sources. The remaining 211 courses combine their exact activity's source facts, same-activity requirement distinctions, evidence-review applications and eight explicit Creditex operating-control scenarios. They do not borrow another activity's quiz or invent extra statutory conditions to reach 25 questions. Linked official reading and independent content review remain essential, particularly for specialist engineering/project methods. Detailed coverage and unresolved source conflicts are recorded at the end of this review.

Every current catalogue activity has its own exact module, including rebates, loans, grants and tariffs. Unknown activities return no module. A generic safety or business course cannot satisfy a newly added unmapped activity. Closed, future and specialist entries have explicit boundary lessons; training does not reopen or independently authorise those pathways. Finishing a course does not authorise another technology, state programme, premises scenario or licensed task.

An internal completion reference must be described as a training record. It is not a government certificate, a trade licence, EEC certification, provider accreditation, a VEEC/STC number or job approval. Business approval and every relevant worker's approval are separate. An owner's pass cannot cover untrained staff. Source or curriculum changes require review of the exact new content and appropriate retraining; a stale completion must not silently qualify revised activity rules.

## Mandatory workflow topics derived from the supplied pack

- Business: strategic partnership and Code of Conduct declaration, public-liability insurance/currency and other applicable insurance, complaint process, disclosure of prior compliance issues, provider review.
- Individual: authorised representative agreement, real identity/photo, task-specific licence/registration and insurance records, activity training and acknowledgement; onsite identity evidence for all actual installers. Decks state a 9 March 2026 start for the selfie requirement; treat that as Creditex's stated operational policy pending reviewer confirmation.
- Before a VEU upgrade: lawful lead provenance, consumer scheme factsheet and rights, activity-specific sizing guidance, product suitability, transparent quote/terms/benefit, identified provider, agreed installation/decommissioning/building-repair scope and consent.
- Per job: exact activity and premises-specific assignment, correct approved system/scenario, complete installation/decommissioning evidence, all relevant licensed-person certificates, invoice/payment/benefit consistency, actual dates, warranty/operation information, waste/refrigerant disposal and retained audit records.
- Both VEU and STC: separate eligibility and assignment rights. A business training pass, activity-selection checkbox or estimate is never evidence that the job is eligible or that certificates were created.
