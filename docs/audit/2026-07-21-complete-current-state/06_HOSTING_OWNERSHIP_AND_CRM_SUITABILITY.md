## Decision

The current combined arrangement is **not suitable as the long-term production home of a business-critical CRM and financial workflow**. Controlled evaluation can continue only under explicit restrictions: payment initiation must remain blocked unless OpenAI confirms that the exact external-checkout design is permitted, unsupported residency or recovery claims must not be made, and the owner must accept that independent D1/R2 administration, export, backup, restore, transfer and workspace-outage continuity are unproven.

This is not a judgment that Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2 or Firebase are inherently unsuitable. Their technology-level verdicts differ from the verdict on the current Sites-managed instances. The decisive combined risks are Sites public-beta and transaction-policy constraints, unavailable Sites data/inference residency, unknown resource/account control, no proven independent recovery, incomplete provider operations and a high operational bus factor.

## D1 suitability and recovery

Cloudflare documents a 10 GB maximum per D1 database and execution of one query at a time per database, with overload handled through a queue: [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/). The current design puts all tenants and product domains in one `DB` binding. There is no `withSession` read-replication use in the repository and no demonstrated production load/capacity envelope.

Cloudflare documents D1 Time Travel for owner-controlled databases, including plan-dependent retention and destructive in-place restore: [D1 Time Travel](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/reference/time-travel/). The current production plan, Time Travel authority and tested restore procedure are `UNKNOWN`.

Cloudflare's export guidance states that export is not supported for databases containing virtual tables and can block requests: [D1 import/export](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/best-practices/import-export-data/). This schema has five FTS5 virtual tables and maintenance triggers. A complete production export therefore requires a provider-supported, rehearsed procedure that explicitly omits/rebuilds FTS artifacts or another supported recovery path. The deployed app console is not that procedure.

## Minimum safe transition plan

1. **Restrict policy-conflicting activation.** Do not enable or expand Stripe/Square transaction paths on Sites until written applicability guidance exists or the routes move to an approved host.
