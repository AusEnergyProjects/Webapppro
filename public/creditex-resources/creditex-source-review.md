# Creditex source notes for activity training

Reviewed for drafting on 18 September 2026. These notes have not yet been approved by an authorised Creditex reviewer. They summarise the supplied training pack and identify corrections. They do not grant a licence, EEC certification, government accreditation, Creditex approval or permission to create certificates.

## Creditex onboarding and individual accountability

The supplied VIC HWS and AC installer training decks, slides 2 and 31/32, require the strategic partnership agreement and Code of Conduct declaration for the business/agent, public-liability certificate of currency and applicable insurance. Each installer must complete the authorised representative agreement and provide relevant licences/insurance and photo ID. Creditex then onboards the installer to its systems and provides the applicable workflow training. The HWS deck slide 33 requires disclosure of prior compliance history and describes audit, inspection, rectification and fraud escalation.

The STC-only deck, slides 2 and 13, calls for training completion/acknowledgement, licence copies, public-liability currency and a selfie. Its mention of NSW ESS is an editorial mistake; STC-only hot-water work falls under federal SRES. A worker must hold the required state/territory licence for the actual task. A business owner's training does not cover another worker's knowledge or licensed scope.

For this platform, course results are internal knowledge records. The required assessment policy is 100% correct with unlimited immediate retakes and 365-day internal validity, subject to authorised Creditex content review. These settings are not statutory competency rules. A new material source/curriculum version or expired credential requires the appropriate review or retraining.

## Consumer conduct and pre-work records

VIC decks slides 3-7 require lawful marketing and lead-source evidence, transparent contracting and a complaints process. Do not acquire leads generated by prohibited unsolicited VEU telemarketing or door knocking. Consent must be documented and not invented retrospectively. Do not imply that your business is the government or ESC.

Before agreement, provide the scheme factsheet, Statement of Rights, relevant hot-water or AC sizing factsheet, product information and warranty/terms. Identify Creditex's accredited-provider role, actual financial benefit, who performs the upgrade, timing, inclusions/exclusions and complaint contacts. Discuss suitability rather than choosing equipment for certificate value. Agree decommissioning, removal and building-repair responsibilities before work. Keep communication and complaint-resolution records.

## Water-heating activities 1 and 3

The supplied HWS deck, checklist and Activity 1/3 assignments support the following corrected topics:

- Activity 1 replaces eligible electric-resistance water heating: 1C installs electric-boosted solar, 1D installs a heat pump. An existing heat pump is not an electric-resistance baseline.
- Activity 3 replaces eligible gas/LPG water heating: 3C installs a heat pump, 3D installs electric-boosted solar. A gas meter elsewhere on the property does not turn an electric water heater into a gas baseline.
- Replacement of boosted solar baselines needs the additional age, failed solar-component and out-of-warranty evidence specified by current rules. Do not assume an eligible baseline from the customer's description.
- Before disconnection: record the real connected old unit, plate, address/context and applicable switchboard evidence. Creditex's HWS deck instructs marking the removed tank with unit/address information to connect its decommissioning evidence to the job.
- Allocate electrical, plumbing, gas and refrigerant work only to appropriately qualified people. If the installer cannot perform gas decommissioning, record the qualified gas fitter and their separate certificate. Do not sign for someone else.
- Safely make the old product permanently unusable; temporary disconnection is not enough. Apply a method suitable for storage or instantaneous equipment. Capture safe isolation/capping and electrical termination where relevant. Remove the old unit where safe/practical, or record the exception and permanent disabling method. Preserve lawful waste/refrigerant disposal records.
- Photograph the actual new hot-water system, stable base, surroundings, piping, lagging and safe pressure-relief discharge. A split heat pump needs both compressor and tank plates. A solar-water-heater installation needs its own relevant system/collector records.
- Use the water-heating factsheet to discuss bedrooms, household demand, climate, recovery and timers. Record a reason for any departure from recommended size.
- Reconcile actual work dates, product details, all actual workers, certificate numbers, invoice and consumer benefit. Retain traceable payment proof showing payer/date/amount and the applicable product warranty. A zero-value invoice does not establish the required contribution.
- A VEU assignment does not transfer federal STC rights; separate eligibility and assignment are needed for a combined claim.

The HWS deck's air-conditioning paragraph and the checklist's repeated description of the old system as a heat pump are copy errors. Do not use them to select a baseline or evidence requirement. The supplied assignment's five-working-day certificate-delivery statement conflicts with the deck's one-month wording. Creditex must confirm the current legal and controlled workflow requirement; the longer deadline is not taught as permission to delay.

## Activity 6 air conditioning

The supplied AC deck and SCH6 checklist require the real baseline heating/cooling scenario, a signed installer/consumer sizing record, room areas, existing/proposed coverage and required heating output. Apply the AC factsheet and actual building/climate conditions. For multi-splits, record every indoor/outdoor component, model, serial, capacities and the current manufacturer/compatibility requirements.

Capture connected old products and their plates before decommissioning. Use qualified people for electrical, plumbing, gas and refrigerant tasks. Record safe permanent disabling and any justified non-removal. After installation record each indoor/outdoor unit, plates, working installation, relevant circuit/main-switch ratings and visible wiring markings required by the checklist. The decks state that, from 9 March 2026, each onsite installer must provide a geotagged selfie with readable ID and the installed product; confirm the accepted privacy-safe capture workflow with Creditex.

The AC deck's instruction to drill a hot-water tank and the SCH6 tank/heat-pump model line are errors. They are not AC installation requirements. The deck's blanket $200 contribution is also incorrect. Apply the category/capacity and actual installation date under [VEU Specifications v25](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0041/795488/Victorian-Energy-Upgrades-Specifications-2018-Version-25.pdf), including changes from 30 September 2026. Do not reimburse the required consumer contribution through cash, goods or services to create the appearance of a qualifying payment.

The Activity 6 assignment and sizing record must match the actual premises, scenario, installed components, licensed workers, dates, certificates, price/contribution and benefit. Obtain genuine informed signatures. Keep warranty, support and lawful disposal/refrigerant records. A recycling certificate need not be in every Creditex upload, but that does not remove the recordkeeping or disposal obligation.

## Federal STC-only hot water

The July 2026 STC checklist and deck require the exact CER-listed new system, appropriate licences, compliance certificates and clear geotagged/date-stamped installed-system/plate/piping/lagging/base evidence. Split heat pumps require tank and compressor plates; every system must be identifiable. More than five systems at one site needs a site plan and serial-number list. Record locations and replacement information for multiple systems.

The controlled assignment must reflect the real owner, site, date, system, legal assignee, entitlement and financial benefit, with no previous assignment or duplicate creation. Creditex's supplied workflow requires owner and witness signatures on or after installation, captured on site. The supplied old generic form includes historic PV deeming choices. Those are not instructions for today's solar-water-heater entitlement.

An invoice may be optional in the Creditex checklist, but the actual CER evidence requirement remains: installer compliance evidence and installer invoice or date-stamped unit photograph, with all required documents/signatures complete before creation. CER creation must occur within 12 months of installation; required documents may be requested for up to five years after creation. A file upload or TLink status does not prove REC Registry creation or approval.

Solar thermal water heaters heat water with collectors and must not be confused with photovoltaic panels. Use their exact registered tank/collector configuration and qualified work. Solar systems over 700 litres need the CER system-owner and system-size statutory declarations and provider review. Heat pumps above 425 litres are ineligible for STCs. Check precise boundaries against current official eligibility/register sources because the supplied checklist uses imprecise 'under' wording.

## Activity 48 and supplied form limitations

No supplied Creditex file is an Activity 48 training deck or assignment. The draft course uses [official insulation industry resources](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/products/insulation-discounts/insulation-requirements-for-industry), specifications and the [DEECA/ESC industry pack](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0026/800945/VEU-Ceiling-Insulation-Industry-Pack.pdf). External EEC CII, ESC participant registration, the provider's exact accreditation and AP-consumer contracting conditions remain independent requirements. A hot-water or AC form cannot be relabelled to certify insulation work.

All three supplied VEEC assignments are residential only. Business/non-residential jobs require their own controlled forms. The Activity 1 form is an older revision. Activity 3 and 6 show a nine-digit value, `105 513 040`, in a field labelled ABN and contain contact details that differ from the decks. Those forms must be corrected and approved by Creditex before live use. Do not guess an ABN or silently alter signed declarations.

Raw installer decks, checklists and ambiguous assignment templates are retained as review sources rather than published here as approved legal forms. Creditex must approve the corrected curriculum, exact controlled documents and replacement evidence workflow before activation.
